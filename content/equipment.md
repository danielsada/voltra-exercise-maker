This is a **hobby page**. I made it to plan my own training with the Beyond Power VOLTRA I. It isn't affiliated
with, endorsed by or sponsored by Beyond Power, and VOLTRA is their trademark. For anything official, such as
mounting, safety limits and firmware features, use [beyond-power.com](https://www.beyond-power.com/) and the
[Beyond Power help centre](https://help.beyond-power.com/). Train safely and follow the manufacturer's instructions.

## Anchor points

The composer asks where you can anchor a Voltra. Beyond Power's mounts are called *AnyMount*: a
[Strap Mount](https://help.beyond-power.com/en/articles/14247113-strap-mount) for posts, pillars and beams,
plus rack, bar and wall mounts ([overview](https://help.beyond-power.com/en/articles/13923993-how-to-mount-voltra-i-with-anymount-accessories-overview)).
Beyond Power describes high, mid and low anchor heights, and the composer uses those plus a pull-up bar.

{{< diagrams >}}
pullup 1 | Pull-up bar | A bar or overhead point you can kneel or stand under. It's where lat pulldowns happen, and it holds both Voltras.
high 1 | Overhead height | Above your head: pushdowns, face pulls, high-to-low chops.
mid 1 | Torso height | Chest to waist: rows, presses, flyes, Pallof presses.
low 1 | Feet height | At or near the floor: curls, deadlifts, kickbacks.
platform 1 | Travel Platform | One Voltra mounted on the platform, and you stand on it.
mid 2 | Two Voltras, side by side | With two Voltras, an anchor height can have two points, one on each side (flyes, presses).
{{< /diagrams >}}

There's no official door anchor. Beyond Power advises against doors and flimsy frames at heavy resistance, so this
site never suggests one.

## The Travel Platform

The [Travel Platform](https://help.beyond-power.com/en/articles/14247119-travel-platform) is a flat floor base
(about 70 × 39 cm). You mount one Voltra on it and stand on the platform to counterbalance the pull, which gives you a
low anchor without a rack. Beyond Power lists deadlift-style hinges, curls, upright rows, low rows, squats,
low-to-high pulls and straight-arm pullovers. Their article only describes one Voltra on it, so the composer
treats the platform as one anchor point.

## Handles and attachments

Names follow Beyond Power's [build-your-bundle](https://www.beyond-power.com/pages/build-your-bundle) page. A
Voltra comes with a carabiner but no handle or mount
([unboxing](https://help.beyond-power.com/en/articles/14247153-unboxing-and-first-time-setup)).

| Attachment | How the composer uses it |
|---|---|
| Basic Handle, Ring Handle | One handle per hand. Exercises that need two handles accept either kind. |
| Ankle Straps | Sold as a pair. Used for kickbacks, leg curls, abduction and knee drives. |
| Functional Rope | Pushdowns, face pulls, hammer curls, crunches and pull-throughs. |
| Squat Harness | A shoulder and ribcage harness ([product page](https://www.beyond-power.com/products/squat-harness)), used for harness squats on the platform. |
| Carbon Bar with Premium Grip, AlumaCore Bar, FusionCore Bar | Treated as **single-cable bars** (one Voltra, centre hook). *Not confirmed by Beyond Power.* |
| Carbon Bar with Hooks | Treated as the **two-Voltra bar** (one Voltra per end hook). *Not confirmed by Beyond Power; inferred from the name.* |
| Darth SaBar | Comes as a centre-hook bar and a dual-hook bar, so it counts as both ([help article](https://help.beyond-power.com/en/articles/11000491-anything-about-darth-sabar-limited-edition)). |

The Rotator Add-on, Heavy-Duty Resistance Cord, Premium Carabiner and Carbon Mobility Stick aren't used by the
composer.

## Two Voltras

Beyond Power's *Twin Mode* pairs two units so that changes on one apply to both
([how to pair](https://help.beyond-power.com/en/articles/10124771-how-do-i-pair-two-voltra-i-devices-in-twin-mode)).
With two Voltras, the composer prefers setups that use both: a handle in each hand from two anchor points, or a bar
across both cables.

## Numbers I didn't use

Beyond Power's pages don't agree on some numbers. The current
[technical specifications](https://www.beyond-power.com/pages/voltra-i-technical-specifications) list 5–230 lb per
unit, while older articles say 5–200 lb. The cable length is given as either 8.5 ft or 9.4 ft. None of the
composer's logic depends on these numbers, so check the official pages for your unit.
