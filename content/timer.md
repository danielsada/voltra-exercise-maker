A workout timer for training with the **Beyond Power VOLTRA I**. **Tabata** runs 20 seconds of work and 10 seconds of
rest for 8 rounds, the original protocol. **Vollaard** is Niels Vollaard's REHIT (reduced-exertion HIIT): an easy
warm-up, two 20-second all-out sprints with three easy minutes between them, and an easy cool-down, about 10 minutes
in all. **Interval** lets you set work, rest, rounds and sets, with a longer rest between sets.
**Workout** runs a workout from the [composer](/) one exercise at a time, showing the setup to use and an animated
drawing, and alternating sides for one-sided exercises.

It beeps on the last three seconds and at every phase change, can vibrate and say exercise names with your device's
own voice, and keeps the screen on while it runs. Space starts and pauses; the arrow keys skip back and forward. Your
settings stay in this browser, and once you've opened the site it also works offline.
